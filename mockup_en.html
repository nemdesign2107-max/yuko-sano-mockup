The Experience

What Is This Experience?

In just 60 minutes, you'll learn the story behind one of Japan's most beloved songs — and sing it together, live, to real piano accompaniment. No experience needed. No Japanese needed, to start.

A short cultural introduction in English → trying the Japanese words together → live piano → singing as a group. That's the whole flow — no rehearsal, no pressure.

Why Furusato

Furusato
My Hometown

Almost every person in Japan learns this song as a child. It's about the seasons, missing home, and the people you love — the quiet feelings that don't always translate into words.

When we explain what the lyrics mean, something changes in the room. That moment — right before everyone sings — is the whole reason this workshop exists.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to speak Japanese?

No. Everything is explained in English first.

I can't sing.

Neither could most of our guests, until they did. This isn't a performance — it's a moment.

Will I feel embarrassed?

Most people say the opposite — it's one of the most relaxed parts of their trip.

How long does it take?

60 minutes.
